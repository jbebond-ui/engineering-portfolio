02 / Process

Less “here is the final object.” More “here is why this decision won.”

  1. 01

    Frame

    Define the problem, constraints, interfaces, and what success actually means.

  2. 02

    Measure

    Replace assumptions with dimensions, loads, data, failure modes, or direct observation.

  3. 03

    Build

    Prototype the highest-risk part first instead of polishing the easiest part.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Test against the requirement, document what failed, then make the next revision better.

ENGINEERING NOTE / 001
“A clean result is useful. A clean explanation of why it works is better.”

This portfolio is designed to show the reasoning behind the work, not just collect finished renders.
