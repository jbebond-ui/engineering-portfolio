Hi, I’m James.

I’m studying mechanical engineering at Drexel University, with an expected graduation in 2027. I like working through the details that turn a promising idea into something people can rely on.

At Apple, I designed an iPhone servicing part, investigated test coverage, and built tools to make production data more useful. At simplehuman, I moved between product teardowns, automated fixtures, and manufacturing partners. At BGE, the work took me from drone payloads to infrastructure-scale mapping. Each experience has given me a different way to approach the next problem.
